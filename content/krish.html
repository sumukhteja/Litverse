The Quiet Path

by Krish

The road bends under the shadow of the elm,
where streetlights fade and stars begin to reign.
A quiet path that leads beyond the realm
of daily noise, of hurry, and of pain.

Each footprint leaves a whisper in the dust,
a silent note in midnight's symphony.
In this dark hour, the simple earth we trust,
and find a peace in our own company.

The wind speaks low of things we used to know,
before the city carved its grids in stone.
It tells us where the quiet waters flow,
and reminds us that we never walk alone.

Krish is a poet and essayist whose work centers on nature, landscape memory, and walks at twilight. He believes that the best ideas arrive when one is walking with no destination in mind.
